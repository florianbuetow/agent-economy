# Completion design

The detailed design is maintained once in `MASTERPLAN-TO-COMPLETE-THE-PROJECT.md`, sections 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8.

Preserve service ownership and SQLite gateway; atomically claim state transitions; persist operation identities, payload hashes and receipts; reconcile unfinished effects independently of business status; freeze Court evidence/verdict before delivery; authorize participants and service capabilities; make all public projections honor sealing; complete restart-safe human/agent journeys.

Do not interpret a final-state compare-and-swap as protection for earlier remote effects, exception cleanup as process-crash recovery, duplicate IDs as equal payloads, or mock judging as evidence of the economic thesis.

Open economic/data-policy decisions are Q-M1 admissibility/privacy, Q-M2 post-v1 price formation, and Q-M3 quality aggregation. Retain existing local-single-operator/manual-funding decisions while resolving those.

Implementation sequencing, migration/backup requirements, acceptance scenarios, release gates and proof records are defined per work package in the root master plan.
